# Replication Readiness Output — Working Draft

Status: outline and initial evidence only  
Logframe activity: A.1.2.4  
Contractual target: one approved RRO

## 1. Executive summary

To be completed after the Akkar pilot. Summarize the problem, users, intervention, evidence, limits, cost, and conditions for replication.

## 2. Akkar context and problem definition

- Farmer and agri-food stakeholder needs.
- Plain versus upland/terraced systems.
- Priority value chains and local constraints.
- Arabic, literacy, connectivity, trust, and channel considerations.
- ESDU's prior relationships and field methods.

## 3. Product and service model

- Website and selected messaging channel.
- Quick, Standard, Deep, and Source-only modes.
- Voice boundary and low-bandwidth design.
- Human referral and field-support model.

## 4. Knowledge model

- Source classes and review lifecycle.
- ESDU internal and public evidence.
- Farmer/local knowledge validation.
- Dynamic sources and expiry.
- Arabic editorial process.

## 5. Technical architecture

- Retrieval, generation, tools, MCP, channels, evidence store.
- Hosting and connectivity profiles.
- Security and data flow.
- Model/provider portability and local-offline option.

## 6. Governance and safety

- Three validation methodologies.
- Roles and approvals.
- High-risk advice boundaries.
- Privacy, consent, retention, and incident handling.

## 7. Performance and adoption evidence

Populate from approved evidence:

- priority-test pass percentage;
- median response time and pilot-load definition;
- controlled availability;
- bilingual retrieval relevance;
- stakeholder enrollment;
- stakeholders trying the model;
- feedback sessions;
- validated high-priority feedback resolution;
- qualitative usefulness and trust findings.

## 8. Cost and operating model

- Model and hosting spend.
- Staff time for knowledge review and field facilitation.
- Messaging/voice costs.
- Monitoring, support, and incident response.
- Cost per active participant and per useful answer.

## 9. Replication pathways

### Adapt to another Lebanese region

- Replace local profile and value-chain modules.
- Recruit regional field-language and subject reviewers.
- Re-run benchmark, safety, latency, and adoption gates.

### Adapt to another institution

- Replace branding, governance owners, source registry, referral network, and data-processing terms.
- Preserve stable content/tool interfaces.

### Offline or low-connectivity deployment

- Local OpenAI-compatible model endpoint.
- Local Whisper and local TTS.
- Synchronized knowledge releases.
- Deferred telemetry with consent and secure upload.

## 10. Conditions that must not be copied blindly

- Akkar crop calendars and market assumptions.
- ESDU project claims outside their documented geography and period.
- Provider model IDs, prices, and data policies.
- Pesticide, veterinary, food-safety, and regulatory guidance.
- Stakeholder engagement methods without local consent and inclusion review.

## 11. Risks and mitigations

Include model error, source staleness, harmful specificity, tool misuse, key spend, provider outage, exclusion, low trust, data leakage, partner-brand misuse, and unsustainable content maintenance.

## 12. Replication decision

Final review should state:

- ready / conditionally ready / not ready;
- minimum team and budget;
- mandatory governance;
- remaining evidence gaps;
- named approval authority and date.
