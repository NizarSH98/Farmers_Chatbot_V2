## 1. Scope and acceptance

This agreement applies throughout the project lifecycle, including research and
design, development, demonstrations, internal and field testing, deployment,
operation, evaluation, maintenance, replication, and retirement. It applies to
the web application, messaging channels, project workspaces, uploaded material,
generated artifacts, retrieval services, tools, and future interfaces that link
to this version.

By creating an account, accepting this agreement, or continuing to use the
service after a material update, you agree to this agreement and acknowledge the
Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the service. A deployment may
also be subject to institutional research, employment, partner, donor, or field
participation terms. Those terms prevail where they impose stronger protections.

## 2. Eligibility and accounts

You must be at least 18 years old, or use the service under approved adult or
institutional supervision. You must use an account you are authorized to use and
provide accurate identity information through the configured sign-in provider.
You are responsible for activity under your session and for signing out on shared
devices. The service never asks for or stores your Google password.

Open registration does not create membership in, employment by, or entitlement
from RAISE, ESDU, AUB, a donor, or any partner. Administrative access is granted
separately and is not based on an email domain alone.

## 3. Purpose of the service

The service provides accessible agricultural, scientific, environmental,
economic, and rural-enterprise decision support. It may retrieve reviewed local
knowledge, search approved sources, perform bounded calculations, ask clarifying
questions, and create plans, budgets, calendars, checklists, or referral briefs.
It is intended to help users understand options and prepare for better decisions;
it does not make decisions on behalf of users.

## 4. AI limitations and user responsibility

AI-generated content can be incomplete, outdated, inapplicable, or wrong even
when it appears confident or includes sources. Citations show the basis used; they
do not guarantee that an answer is correct for a particular farm, animal,
business, locality, season, or person.

You must independently verify important outputs. The service does not replace a
qualified agronomist, veterinarian, physician, laboratory, engineer, accountant,
lawyer, financial adviser, pesticide authority, food-safety authority, or
emergency service. Never delay emergency action while waiting for the service.

Do not rely on the service alone for pesticide selection or dosage, veterinary
prescriptions, diagnosis, food or water safety, structural or electrical work,
legal compliance, credit, insurance, or high-value financial decisions. Prices,
regulations, registrations, weather, disease alerts, and market conditions can
change and require current local verification. No output guarantees yield,
profit, eligibility, approval, safety, or regulatory compliance.

## 6. Your content and permissions

You retain rights you hold in questions, instructions, files, images, feedback,
and other material you provide. You grant the operator and approved processors a
limited permission to host, copy, transform, retrieve, and transmit that content
only as needed to operate, secure, support, evaluate, and improve the service as
described in the Privacy Policy.

Upload only material you are authorized to use. Do not upload confidential

Uploaded files are user-provided context, not approved project knowledge. They
